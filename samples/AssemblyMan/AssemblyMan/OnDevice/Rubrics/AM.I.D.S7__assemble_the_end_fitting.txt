assemble_the_end_fitting — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed hose end with its socket and nipple assembled as an MS end fitting visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Socket is threaded onto the hose end, gripping the hose over its full socket length
2. A visible gap separates the nipple hex from the socket face, not metal-to-metal contact
3. Nipple is installed into the socket and its hex remains exposed for wrench access
4. Cover is removed only in a band at the fitting end, exposing wire braid beneath
5. Hose cover remains intact and undamaged beyond the stripped band
6. Hose enters the fitting straight, with no kink or flattening at the socket mouth

Critical defects
- Wire braid strands are cut, splayed or unwound in the stripped band
- Socket is on the hose with an open bore and no nipple installed
- Nipple hex is bottomed flush against the socket with no gap
- Inner tube is split, torn or extruded out of the fitting
- Hose is kinked or collapsed where it enters the socket

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
