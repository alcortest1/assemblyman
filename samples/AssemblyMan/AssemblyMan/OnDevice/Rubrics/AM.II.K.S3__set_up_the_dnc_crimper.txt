set_up_the_dnc_crimper — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed DNC crimper set up with its turret installed and wire-size selection made visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Turret is seated flat and fully down on the crimper tool face
2. Turret set screws are present and seated in their holes, not backed out
3. Cotter key is installed through the handle selector dial
4. A numbered position on the selector dial is aligned with the handle index mark
5. One turret crimp hole is centered over the tool opening, not between positions
6. Crimper jaws are open and the crimp hole is empty, no contact inserted

Critical defects
- Turret sits cocked or raised off the tool face
- A turret set screw hole is visibly empty
- Dial cotter key hole is empty, dial unrestrained
- Turret is unlatched, with no crimp hole over the opening
- Turret keying tang or crimp hole is visibly broken or deformed

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
