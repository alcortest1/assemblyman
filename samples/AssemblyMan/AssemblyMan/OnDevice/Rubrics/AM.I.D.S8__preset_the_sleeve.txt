preset_the_sleeve — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed flareless tube connection with the sleeve preset, tube nut tightened onto the fitting in a vise visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Tube nut is threaded onto the fitting body, drawn up tight against it
2. Fitting is clamped in a vise, held squarely with the tube in line
3. Tube enters the nut straight, with no visible bend or cocking at the joint
4. Tube end is square to its axis where visible, with no ragged burr or chips

Critical defects
- Tube is visibly collapsed, crushed or necked down at the nut
- Tube end is bare against the fitting with no nut installed
- Nut is cracked, split, or its threads are visibly stripped

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
