route_the_line — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed safety-wire route pattern marked for bends and measured for tubing length visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. A single continuous length of safety wire is present as the route pattern.
2. Felt-tip marker marks are visible on the wire at bend locations.
3. Marker marks are visible at both ends of the wire.
4. The wire lies straight and flat alongside a tape measure or scale in frame.
5. The wire's zero end is set at the tape measure's zero or a stated start graduation.
6. The wire shows smooth, rounded bend forms rather than sharp folds where bends are marked.

Critical defects
- The safety wire is severed into two or more separate pieces.
- A sharp kink or crimp interrupts the wire route path.
- The wire in frame carries no marker marks at bends or ends.
- The wire is coiled or looped back over itself, not one route path.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
