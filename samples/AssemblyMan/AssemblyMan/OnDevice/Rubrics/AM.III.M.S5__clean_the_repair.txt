clean_the_repair — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed cleaned and measured blend repair area on a metal propeller blade visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Repair area is free of fine metallic dust and sanding debris
2. Blended depression is visible on the blade, its surface smooth with no scratch pattern of coarse filing
3. A steel scale or depth gauge is positioned on the repair area in frame
4. Blade surface around the repair shows no solvent-smeared residue or loose rag fibers

Critical defects
- A visible crack runs through or beside the blended area
- Blade edge is gouged or notched with a sharp, unblended step
- Loose metal filings or rag left resting on the blade

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
