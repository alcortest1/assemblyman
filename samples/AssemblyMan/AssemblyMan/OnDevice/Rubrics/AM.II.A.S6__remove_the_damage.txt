remove_the_damage — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed damage removal — cutout or stop-drilled crack with smoothed, deburred edges visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Damage site shows a cut-out opening or drilled holes at the crack ends
2. Cut edges appear smooth and rounded, free of burrs, jagged metal or tool marks
3. Any rectangular cutout has rounded corners rather than sharp square corners
4. No patch, doubler, filler or rivets installed at the opening

Critical defects
- Crack continues past the drilled hole into surrounding skin
- Cutout edge shows tears, jagged burrs or torn metal
- Fresh gouges or scratches from cutting extend into surrounding skin

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.

Source basis

- Notes: Handbook p. 4-96 gives corner radius no smaller than 1/4 inch; the task list cites 1/2 inch. Neither dimension is graded, as radius requires measurement.; Stop-hole diameter (3/32 inch), corner radius and grit progression are not settleable from a photograph and are not graded.; The photograph cannot establish structural integrity or that all damage was removed below the surface.
