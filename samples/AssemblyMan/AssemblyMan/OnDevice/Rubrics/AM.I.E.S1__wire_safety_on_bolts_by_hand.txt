wire_safety_on_bolts_by_hand — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed hand-twisted safety wire installation joining two bolt heads, with pigtail visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. A single wire connects two bolt holes, with twisted wire between the bolt heads.
2. Twists between bolts are even, with 6-8 wraps per inch measured against a rule in frame.
3. The wire loop lies down against the bolt head, not riding up over it.
4. Pigtail at the end shows defined twists and is bent back toward the bolt head.

Critical defects
- Wire between bolts hangs slack or visibly loose.
- Pigtail end sticks straight out unbent as a snag.
- Wire is kinked, frayed, or broken along its run.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
