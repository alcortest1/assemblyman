create_the_patch_doubler — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed cut sheet metal patch doubler for a flush patch repair visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. A single flat sheet metal doubler is cut free and separate from stock.
2. Doubler is visibly larger than the prepared skin opening on all sides.
3. Corners are rounded, not left as square points.
4. Cut edges are continuous and straight, without shear steps or torn slivers.

Critical defects
- Doubler is smaller than the opening it must cover.
- Doubler is buckled, cracked, or torn at a cut edge.
- A corner is cut as a sharp square notch or overcut past the edge.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
