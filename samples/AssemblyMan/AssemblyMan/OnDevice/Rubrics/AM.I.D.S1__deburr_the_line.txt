deburr_the_line — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed deburred and resized tubing ends on the cut rigid tube visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Tube end opening is unobstructed and visible in frame for edge inspection
2. Outer edge of the tube end shows a smooth, evenly broken edge with no raised burr ring
3. Inner edge of the bore at the tube end shows no curled or protruding burr
4. Tube ends remain plain and unflared, with no fitting installed
5. Tube remains straight, with no bend formed at this stage
6. Outer surface near the end is wiped free of fluid slurry so the edge can be seen

Critical defects
- Torn or curled metal lip protrudes from the tube end
- Tube end is visibly crushed or flattened out of round
- A split or crack runs from the tube end along the wall
- Loose metal chips or sanding grit sit at the tube opening

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
