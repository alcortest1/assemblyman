remove_the_rivet — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed riveted patch with one rivet drilled and punched out, leaving a vacated hole visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. One rivet location in the riveted patch is empty, with no rivet in it
2. No sheared rivet head or shank fragment remains lodged in the vacated hole
3. Sheet metal immediately around the vacated hole is free of drill gouges or scoring
4. Skin and patch around the vacated hole lie flat, without dishing or bulging
5. Remaining rivets in the pattern are still installed with heads intact
6. Patch and base sheet stay in contact at the vacated hole, with no gap

Critical defects
- Crack or tear radiates from the edge of the vacated hole
- Vacated hole is visibly elongated or irregular in shape
- Rivet shank remains in the hole with its head sheared off
- Sheet around the vacated hole is dented or deformed by hammer blows
- Rivet heads adjacent to the vacated hole are gouged or sheared

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.

Source basis
- Procedure: tasks/AM.II.A.S6/procedure.md § Remove the Rivet
- Manual: FAA-H-8083-31B ch. 4 p. 4-85; FAA-H-8083-31B ch. 4 p. 4-92
- Notes: Neither source gives dimensional limits for a vacated rivet hole; only visibly irregular, elongated or cracked conditions are gradeable from the photograph.; The photograph cannot establish the drill or punch diameters used, drilling depth, or that the hole remains at nominal size; those are excluded from the criteria.; Hole roundness is graded only as gross visible irregularity, not against original size, which would require measurement.
