rivet_layout — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed rivet layout marked out on the patch and mating material visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Marker edge-distance line runs parallel to and set in from each patch edge
2. Edge-distance lines are present along every edge of the riveted overlap
3. Rivet dots sit on the marked edge-distance lines, inboard of the material edge
4. Rivet dots appear on both the patch and the mating skin or component
5. Dots along each line are evenly spaced by eye, none bunched or doubled
6. No holes are drilled at any marked dot location

Critical defects
- A marked dot lies on or past the material edge, no margin
- Marked dots fall within the cut-out opening instead of the overlap
- The patch in frame carries no layout marks on its surface
- Rivet dots scattered with no discernible row or line alignment
- Visible crack, tear or deep gouge in the marked patch material

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
