rivet_the_material — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed driven rivet joining the patch to the skin, with its formed shop head visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. A formed shop head is visible on the shank side of the joined sheets.
2. The manufactured rivet head sits flush against the skin, not standing proud.
3. A scale, dividers, or rule appears in frame against the shop head for measurement.
4. Sheets are drawn tight together at the rivet with no visible gap.

Critical defects
- Shop head is bent or clinched sideways off the shank axis.
- Skin around the rivet is dented or gouged by the rivet set.
- Rivet shank is visibly bent or the shank buckled between sheets.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.

Source basis
- Procedure: tasks/AM.II.A.S6/procedure.md § Rivet the Material
- Manual: FAA-H-8083-31B ch. 4 p. 4-92
- Notes: Procedure gives preferred shop head 0.5D height and 1.5D width; a photograph cannot establish these dimensions, so only the presence of a measuring reference is graded.; Rivet diameter, material, and hole fit are not determinable from the image.
