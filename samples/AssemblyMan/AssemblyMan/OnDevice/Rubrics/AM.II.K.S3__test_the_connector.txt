test_the_connector — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed mated electrical connector pair with its terminated wire, after the continuity and position check visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Both connector halves are joined together as one mated assembly.
2. Mated shells are drawn fully together with no gap between their mating faces.
3. Wire exits the rear of the connector with no bare conductor showing at the entry.
4. Wire insulation is continuous and unbroken along the visible length to the connector.
5. Wire remains seated in the connector and is not pulled back or hanging loose.
6. Connector shell, coupling ring and rubber insert are intact, uncracked and undeformed.

Critical defects
- Connector halves rest cocked or half-engaged with an open gap between shells.
- The wire has pulled free of the connector rear and hangs loose.
- Exposed copper strands are visible outside the connector rear grommet.
- A contact pin on a connector face is visibly bent, crushed or broken off.
- The connector shell or rubber insert is split, gouged or torn.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.

Source basis
