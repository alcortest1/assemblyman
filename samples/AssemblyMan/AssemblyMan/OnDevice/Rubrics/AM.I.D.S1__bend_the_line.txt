bend_the_line — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed rigid tube with its hand-formed bend(s), ends not yet flared visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Tube shows at least one completed bend formed along its length
2. Bend outer wall is smooth, free of wrinkles or corrugations
3. Bend region shows no kink or sharp collapse at the throat
4. Tube remains straight in one plane on each side of the bend, not twisted
5. Felt-tip bend layout mark visible on tube near the bend
6. Tube ends are open and unflared, consistent with flaring not yet performed

Critical defects
- Wrinkled or corrugated bend visible on the tube
- Tube is kinked or collapsed flat at the bend
- Crack or split visible in the bend wall
- Tube is severed or buckled through at the bend

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
