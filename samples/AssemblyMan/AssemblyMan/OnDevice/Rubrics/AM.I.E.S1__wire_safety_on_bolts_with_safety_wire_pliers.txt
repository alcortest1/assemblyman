wire_safety_on_bolts_with_safety_wire_pliers — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed safety wire installation joining two bolt heads, twisted with safety wire pliers and finished with a pigtail visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. A single continuous twisted wire pair links two bolt holes.
2. Twists between the bolts are even and continuous, with no untwisted slack span.
3. Twist density measured against a rule or scale in frame shows 6-8 wraps per inch.
4. Pigtail end is bent back toward the bolt head, not left standing out.

Critical defects
- The wire loop rides up over the bolt head, leaving a slack loop.
- A wire strand is visibly broken, kinked, or frayed.
- The cut pigtail end stands straight out unbent as a snag hazard.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
