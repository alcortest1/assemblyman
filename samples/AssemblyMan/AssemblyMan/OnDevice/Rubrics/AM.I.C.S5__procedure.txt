procedure — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed weight-and-balance worksheet computing CG after an equipment change visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Weight, arm, and moment entries are filled in for each weighed location.
2. Summed totals appear at the bottom of the weight and moment columns.
3. A CG value in inches is recorded on the worksheet.
4. A written CG range appears with a marked in-range or out-of-range conclusion.

Critical defects
- The arm column carries a summed total at its bottom.
- No moment column or moment values appear on the worksheet.
- CG value lies outside the written range but is marked acceptable.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
