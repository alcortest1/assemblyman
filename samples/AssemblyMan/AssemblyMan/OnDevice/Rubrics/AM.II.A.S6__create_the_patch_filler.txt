create_the_patch_filler — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed cut and filed patch filler fitted into the skin opening visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. A separate filler piece is present, cut to the shape of the skin opening.
2. The filler sits in the opening with its face level with the surrounding skin.
3. The gap between filler edge and opening edge is narrow and even all around.
4. Filler edges show no burrs, ragged cut marks, or unsanded scratches.

Critical defects
- Filler is visibly smaller than the opening, leaving a wide open gap.
- Filler edge is torn, cracked, or gouged from cutting.
- Filler is buckled or bent, standing proud of the skin surface.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
