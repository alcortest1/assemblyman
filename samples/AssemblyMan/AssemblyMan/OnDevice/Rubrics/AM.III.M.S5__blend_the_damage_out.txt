blend_the_damage_out — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed blended and fine-sanded repair area on a metal propeller blade visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Damaged area is blended into a rounded depression with no sharp corners or V-shaped notches
2. Blended area transitions gradually into surrounding blade surface with no abrupt step visible
3. Repair surface shows fine uniform sanding scratches, not coarse file marks or gouges
4. No burrs, raised lips or torn metal remain at the perimeter of the blend

Critical defects
- A crack is visible in or beside the blended area
- Blend cuts across the full width of leading or trailing edge
- Blade is bent, gouged deeply, or shows a hole through the surface

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
