drill_the_holes — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed drilled and deburred rivet holes in the skin, doubler and filler of a patch repair visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Each rivet layout mark on the skin has a drilled hole through it.
2. Drilled holes appear round and uniform, not elongated, torn or triangular.
3. Visible hole edges show no burr ring or raised metal.
4. Doubler and filler holes align with skin holes, shown by cleco pins in place.

Critical defects
- A drilled hole breaks out through a part edge or cutout.
- Skin or doubler is cracked or torn between adjacent holes.
- Metal around a hole is dished, gouged or deformed by chuck contact.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
