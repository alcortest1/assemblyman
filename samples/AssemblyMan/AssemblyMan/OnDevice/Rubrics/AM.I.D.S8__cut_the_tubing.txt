cut_the_tubing — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed cut and deburred length of rigid tubing, before any sleeve or nut is installed visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Tubing is fully severed into a separate piece with the cut end free.
2. Cut end retains a round cross-section, not pinched flat or oval.
3. Bore at the cut end is open and clear of any raised burr ring.
4. Outside edge of the cut end is smooth, with no raised curled lip.
5. Tube wall adjacent to the cut is free of gouges or deep score grooves.
6. Tube end is bare: no sleeve and no B-nut installed on it yet.

Critical defects
- Cut end is crushed flat or collapsed out of round.
- Tube shows a kink, wrinkle or flattened section along its length.
- Cut end carries a torn, jagged tab of partially severed metal.
- Bore of the tube contains loose metal chips or shavings.
- Deep spiral or circumferential cuts scar the tube wall away from the cut.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
