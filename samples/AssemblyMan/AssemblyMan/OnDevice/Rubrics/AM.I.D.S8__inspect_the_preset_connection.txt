inspect_the_preset_connection — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed preset flareless tube end with its sleeve and B-nut, disconnected from the fitting for inspection visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Tube assembly is separated from the fitting; no fitting engaged in the B-nut
2. B-nut and sleeve both remain on the tube end
3. Tube end protrudes visibly beyond the end face of the sleeve pilot
4. A rule, scale, or caliper in frame spans the sleeve pilot face to the tube end
5. Sleeve cutting edge bite mark is visible as a ring impression on the tube
6. Tube end bore remains open and round, not pinched shut

Critical defects
- Tube is split or cracked at the sleeve cutting edge
- Tube end is flush with or recessed behind the sleeve pilot
- Tube is crushed flat or closed at the sleeve cut
- Tube end is bare, with no sleeve between the B-nut and the tube end
- Tube is sharply kinked or bent immediately behind the sleeve

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.

Source basis

- Manual: FAA-H-8083-30B ch. 9 p. 9-6; FAA-H-8083-30B ch. 9 p. 9-8
- Notes: Procedure sheet says 0.015" for corrosion-resistant steel; handbook p. 9-6 text says 'steel tubing' while Figure 9-14 on p. 9-8 says 'corrosion-resistant steel tubing'. Neither clearance is judged here.; A photograph cannot establish the 3/32"-1/8" protrusion value, sleeve pilot clearance, tube material, or that the sleeve moves only in rotation; only the presence and position of the parts and visible damage are graded.
