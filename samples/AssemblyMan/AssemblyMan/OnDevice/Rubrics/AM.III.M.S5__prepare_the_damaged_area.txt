prepare_the_damaged_area — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed damaged metal propeller blade area cleaned, measured and outlined for repair visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Damage on the blade is enclosed by a closed dye or marker outline
2. Outline is a smooth contoured shape tapering into the damage, no sharp corners
3. Blade surface inside and around the outline is clean, free of oil, dirt and debris
4. Metal inside the outline is intact, with no filing or sanding cuts present

Critical defects
- A crack extends from the damaged area
- Part of the damage lies outside the marked outline
- Marker or dye covers the damage, hiding it from inspection

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
