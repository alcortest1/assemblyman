cut_the_hose — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed flexible hose cut to length with the cut end exposed and tape removed visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Hose is severed completely into separate pieces at the cut location.
2. Cut face is square across the hose, not sloped or angled.
3. Cut end is flat, without ragged or torn cover material.
4. Masking tape is removed from the cut end, leaving the hose surface bare.

Critical defects
- Wire braid is frayed and splayed outward from the cut face.
- Cut face is visibly crushed or flattened out of round.
- Cut is partial, with the hose still joined at the cut line.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
