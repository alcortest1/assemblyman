tie_a_starting_knot_using_the_single_cord_lacing_method — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed starting knot on the wire bundle: clove hitch secured by a double-looped overhand knot visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. A cord hitch is drawn tight around the wire bundle at one clear location.
2. The knot lies on a clear section of bundle, clear of clamps or connectors.
3. A second knot of crossed loops sits against the hitch, not spaced away from it.
4. Cord tails are cut off short rather than left as long loose lengths.

Critical defects
- Cord lies loose around the bundle, standing away from the wires.
- Only one knot is present, with no locking knot against the hitch.
- Wire insulation is cut, nicked, or gouged near the knot.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
