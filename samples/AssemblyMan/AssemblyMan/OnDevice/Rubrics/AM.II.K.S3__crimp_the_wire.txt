crimp_the_wire — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed crimped pin contact terminated on a single stripped wire, removed from the crimper visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. One pin contact is crimped onto the end of a single wire.
2. Crimp indentation marks are visible on the contact's wire barrel.
3. Wire insulation stops outside the barrel; bare conductor enters the barrel.
4. No loose conductor strands protrude outside or alongside the crimp barrel.
5. The pin's mating end is straight, unflattened, and free of tool marks.
6. The crimped contact is loose and not seated in a connector cavity.

Critical defects
- The wire is separated from the pin contact.
- The crimp barrel is split, cracked, or crushed through.
- The pin mating end is visibly bent or flattened.
- Two wires are terminated in the one crimp barrel.
- Insulation is captured inside the barrel with no bare conductor entering it.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
