procedure — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed worksheet showing the ballast weight calculation and required ballast location visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Aircraft total weight is written on the worksheet in pounds
2. An out-of-range CG distance in inches is written as a subtraction of two CG values
3. A ballast location is written as a station or arm in inches
4. The ballast formula appears with weight times CG shift divided by distance from ballast location to limit
5. A final ballast weight answer is written in pounds

Critical defects
- Division and multiplication are transposed in the written formula
- The written arithmetic gives a result other than the shown numbers produce
- The final answer is blank or has no pounds unit

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
