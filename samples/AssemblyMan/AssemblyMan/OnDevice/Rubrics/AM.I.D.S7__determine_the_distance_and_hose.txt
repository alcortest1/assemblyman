determine_the_distance_and_hose — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed flexible hose marked with masking tape for length, with its matching MS fitting assembly visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Masking tape band is wrapped around the hose, encircling it fully
2. Tape band edges are square to the hose axis, not spiralling
3. A tape measure or rule appears in frame with the hose
4. Hose lay line dash number and a fitting dash number are both legible

Critical defects
- Hose is already cut or slit at the tape mark
- Hose shows a kink, crushed section, or cover cut through to braid
- Fitting dash number visibly differs from the hose lay line dash number

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
