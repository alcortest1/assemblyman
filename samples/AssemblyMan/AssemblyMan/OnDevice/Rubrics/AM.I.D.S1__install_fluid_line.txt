install_fluid_line — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed rigid line installed and tightened on the aircraft fittings visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Line is connected at both ends, a B-nut engaged on each fitting
2. Each B-nut is drawn up to its fitting with no thread gap at the joint
3. Tube enters each fitting straight and square, not sprung sideways to reach it
4. Bend in the installed line is smooth and round, free of wrinkles or flat sides
5. Tube and B-nut exterior surfaces show no wrench gouges or crushed flats
6. Fitting joints and surrounding tube surface are dry, no fluid film present

Critical defects
- One end of the line hangs free, no B-nut on its fitting
- A B-nut sits loose with exposed threads and an open joint
- The bend is kinked or flattened with a visible flat side
- Fluid beads or runs from a B-nut joint
- Tube is visibly bowed or twisted to force it onto the fittings

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.

Source basis

- Notes: Final tightening (high torque rise plus one-third turn) and use of a backup wrench cannot be settled from a photograph; only the resulting seated joint is graded.; Pressure integrity, internal condition, tube material and wall thickness are not visible; a dry joint in the photograph does not establish a leak-free system.; Percent flattening at the bend requires measurement; only visible kinks, wrinkles or flat sides are graded.
