proof_test_the_hose_assembly — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed flexible hose assembly capped and pressurized on the hydraulic hose test stand visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. One end fitting of the hose assembly is coupled to the test stand port.
2. The opposite end fitting carries a cap or plug, with no open port.
3. A test stand pressure gauge is visible in frame with the assembly.
4. The gauge pointer is off zero, showing the assembly under pressure.
5. Hose cover, socket-to-hose junctions and both fittings appear dry, with no fluid weeping.
6. The pressurized hose lies in a smooth run, free of kinks or sharp bends.

Critical defects
- Fluid is spraying or dripping from a fitting or the hose cover.
- The hose end opposite the stand is open, with no cap or plug fitted.
- The hose cover is burst, blistered, or wire braid is protruding.
- A socket has pulled away from the hose, leaving a gap.
- The pressurized hose is kinked or collapsed at a sharp bend.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
