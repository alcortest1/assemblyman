procedure — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed completed FAA Form 337 for a major repair or alteration visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Block 1 shows aircraft make, model, serial number and registration number entered
2. Block 2 shows the registered owner's name and address entered
3. Block 4 has one box marked, either repair or alteration, not both
4. Block 8 text names the approved data used for the work
5. Block 6 shows a signature and a completion date

Critical defects
- Block 8 is blank, with no description of work performed
- Both repair and alteration boxes in Block 4 are marked
- Block 6 signature line on the form is blank

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
