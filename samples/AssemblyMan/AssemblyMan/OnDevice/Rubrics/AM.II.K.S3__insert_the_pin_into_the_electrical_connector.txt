insert_the_pin_into_the_electrical_connector — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed electrical connector with the crimped pin termination installed in its contact cavity visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. A crimped contact is installed in a contact cavity of the connector
2. The contact body is seated flush with or below the connector insert face, not standing proud
3. Only one wire enters the connector at the occupied cavity
4. The installer tool is clear of the connector, not left in the cavity
5. Wire insulation is continuous up to the connector rear grommet, no bare conductor showing
6. The connector insert/grommet around the occupied cavity is intact, no tears or gouges

Critical defects
- Contact protrudes above the insert face instead of sitting in the cavity
- Contact and wire lie loose outside the connector, no contact in any cavity
- Pin contact visibly bent, crushed, or its tip broken off
- Two wires occupy the same contact cavity
- Connector rubber insert visibly torn or gouged at the cavity

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
