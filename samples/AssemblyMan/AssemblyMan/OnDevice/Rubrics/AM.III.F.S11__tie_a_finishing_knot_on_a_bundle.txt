tie_a_finishing_knot_on_a_bundle — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed finishing knot terminating a laced wire bundle visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. A finishing knot is present at the end of the lacing run, terminating the bundle.
2. The finishing knot encircles the full bundle, with cord passing twice around it.
3. Multiple over-and-under locking wraps are visible on the taut lacing past the finishing knot.
4. The cord tail is trimmed short, with no long loose strand hanging from the knot.

Critical defects
- The finishing knot is open or unravelled, with cord hanging free of the bundle.
- Cord passes around only part of the bundle, leaving wires outside the lacing.
- Wire insulation is cut, nicked or gouged adjacent to the finishing knot.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
