identify_the_damage — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed marked-out damage area on the sheet metal part, measured and outlined prior to removal visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Damage such as a crack, hole, dent or tear is visible on the part
2. A marker line forms a continuous closed boundary around the damage
3. All visible damage lies inside the marked boundary, none touching the line
4. A steel scale is in frame laid across the damage for height and length
5. The part is still whole: no cut, sawn or drilled openings in the marked area
6. Marker line contrasts with the surface and is legible along its full length

Critical defects
- Material is cut, sawn or drilled away inside the marked outline
- The marker line runs across or through the visible damage
- The marked outline is broken, leaving the damage not enclosed
- Visible damage extends outside the marked outline
- Marker outline is drawn on an area containing no visible damage

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
