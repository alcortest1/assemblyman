flare_the_line — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed flared rigid tube end with its sleeve and B-nut installed visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. A cone-shaped flare, wider than the tube body, is formed at the tube end.
2. A B-nut and a sleeve are on the tube behind the flare.
3. Sleeve pulled to the flare shows a thin blue ring around the flared end.
4. Flare surface is free of cracks, splits, nicks or scratches.

Critical defects
- The flare is split or cracked at its rim.
- The tube end is bare, with no sleeve or B-nut on it.
- The B-nut threads face away from the flared end.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
