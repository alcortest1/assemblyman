determine_the_distance_of_lacing — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed gathered wire bundle with its measuring tape and the cut length of lacing cord visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Loose wires are gathered into one parallel bundle rather than lying separately
2. A tape measure lies alongside the bundle with its graduated scale visible
3. A cut length of lacing cord is present in the frame, free of any spool
4. The cut cord, laid or coiled beside the bundle, is visibly longer than the bundle run
5. The cord is one continuous piece with two cut ends, not several fragments
6. No knot, hitch or cord wrap is present on the bundle

Critical defects
- Wire insulation is cut, nicked or stripped bare on the bundle
- The cut cord is visibly shorter than the bundle it will lace
- Wires splay and cross in random directions, forming no bundle
- Cord is severed into multiple short unusable pieces

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
