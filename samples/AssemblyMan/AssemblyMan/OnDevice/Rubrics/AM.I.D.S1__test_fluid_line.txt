test_fluid_line — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed rigid line connected to the pressure tester inside the enclosed test box for leak check visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Fabricated rigid line is coupled to a pressure tester fitting by its B-nut.
2. Rigid line lies inside the enclosed pressure test box, not outside it.
3. B-nut is threaded onto the tester fitting with no visible thread gap.
4. Tube surface, sleeve base, and B-nut are dry of pooled or running hydraulic fluid.

Critical defects
- Hydraulic fluid runs or beads at the sleeve base or B-nut joint.
- Tube shows a split, crack, or bulge along its length.
- Line is coupled to the tester but lies outside the enclosed box.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
