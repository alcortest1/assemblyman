prepare_the_wire — VLM GRADING CRITERIA

Assess the completed wire cut, stripped and inserted into the pin contact, before crimping visible in the image. Evaluate each criterion independently as PASS or FAIL.

Criteria
1. Bare stranded conductor is visible at the prepared wire end, insulation removed cleanly.
2. Bare conductor is inserted into the pin's wire barrel opening.
3. Insulation ends at or just short of the barrel entry, with no long bare gap.
4. All strands are gathered inside the barrel; none splayed or standing outside it.
5. Only one wire enters the pin contact.
6. Pin barrel is round and free of crimp indentations or tool marks.

Critical defects
- Pin barrel is empty; the stripped wire lies beside it, uninserted.
- Insulation is pushed inside the barrel, no bare conductor entering the pin.
- Strands are visibly cut, broken or fanned outside the barrel mouth.
- The pin is bent, crushed or split along the barrel.
- Two wires are inserted into the single pin contact.

Overall decision
Overall PASS requires every required criterion to pass and no critical defect. Mark an unobservable required criterion “FAIL — not demonstrated in image.” Evaluate only visible evidence and do not infer hidden measurements, internal damage, torque, pressure, or test results.
