### Step Instructions

The form 337 is a document to keep track of significant changes to an aircraft for an accurate configuration history. The AC 43.9-1G gives thorough instructions for completing the form.

1. Confirm form 337 requirement1.
2. Collect approved data2.
3. Fill out block 13.
4. Fill out block 24.

6. Fill out block 56.
7. Fill out block 67.
8. Fill out block 88.
9. Determine who receives the document9.
10. Write a logbook entry10.

### Senior Mechanic Notes

1. This form is required when the work performed meets the “major” repair or alteration requirement outlined in 14 CFR part 43 appendix A.
2. Applicable reference data to complete the work must be used such as a STC, technical drawing, or a process specific manual.
3. Use the information from the aircraft’s data plate for its make, model, serial number, and registration number.
4. Find the aircraft’s registration records for the owner’s name and address.
5. Select the correct action performed, either repair or alteration.
6. Fill in the product’s information such as make, model, and serial number.
7. Fill in A and B with the person’s information who performed the work. Verify the conformity statement. Sign only when work has been completed.
8. Explain clearly what was done, how it was done, what approved data was used, and an airworthiness statement. If more space is needed, note where those pages are.
9. Keep the original form 337 with the aircraft records. Provide a copy to the owner and send a copy to the FAA within 48 hours of completion.
10. The form 337 does not replace a logbook entry. Include a write up for the action performed with reference to the form 337 and a statement for return to service.
