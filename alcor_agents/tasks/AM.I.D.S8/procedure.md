## Cut the Tubing

### Step Instructions

1. Measure needed length of tubing.
2. Cut the tubing1.
3. Deburr the tubing ends2-3.

### Senior Mechanic Notes

1. Open the rolling jaws just wide enough to set the rigid line in. Align the marked line and sharp cutting wheel precisely, hold in place with one hand. Use your other hand to twist the knob, tightening the cutting wheel to the tubing slightly snug. Hold the tubing firm while rotating the cutting tool around it. The cutting tool will loosen after a few rotations. Continue re-tightening and rotating the cutting tool until cleanly cut through tubing.
2. Insert deburring tool tip into the end of the tubing. Press tubing firmly into the tool while twisting it in a motion similar to sharpening a pencil. Perform 3-4 twists at a time and check the inside diameter is smooth and the original size returned.
3. Place a sheet of sandpaper on a flat surface and apply a drop of hydraulic fluid in the center. Hold the tubing end perpendicular and press it firmly into the area lubricated with the hydraulic fluid, moving it in a continuous figure-8 pattern on the sandpaper until the end is smooth. Then, wrap the sandpaper around the tubing end and twist the tubing, like sharpening a pencil, to remove any remaining burrs.
