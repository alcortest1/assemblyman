## AM.I.C.S5 - Calculate Weight and Balance for an Aircraft After an Equipment Change.

> Converted from the source .docx skill sheet. Trailing digits in step text (e.g. `ends2-3`) are cross-references to the numbered **Senior Mechanic Notes** in the same section, and are preserved verbatim.

### Step Instructions

An aircraft's weight and balance is critical for safe flight. The weight of an object on the aircraft at a distance from the datum, its (arm), will cause a moment.

Weight x Arm = Moment

If the aircraft was suspended from a string while maintaining a level flight condition, this location would be its center of gravity (CG). Each aircraft has a CG range, calculating its weight and balance will confirm this range is not exceeded in the aircraft's current loaded condition.

1. Identify the weight at the landing gear locations1.
2. Identify the arms of each weight2.
3. Multiply the weight by the arm3.
4. Find the total weight4.
5. Find the total moment5.
6. Divide the total moment by the total weight6.
7. Determine if the CG is in range7.

### Senior Mechanic Notes

1. Commonly, aircraft are weighed on scales at 3 locations, either by load cells installed on jacks or roll-on scales at the landing gear. Read each scale for their individual weight; Tricycle gear aircraft will identify these as right-hand and left-hand main landing gear and nose gear (e.g., 1,250 RMLG; 1,200 LMLG; 1,125 NG).
2. The arm of each weight is its distance from the datum in inches. If a location is in front of the datum, this value will be a negative number (e.g., 120" RMLG; 120" LMLG; -45" NG).
3. Multiply each weight by its own arm (e.g., 1,250 x 120 = 150,000; 1,200 x 120 = 144,000; 1,125 x -45 = -50,625).
4. Add each weight together (e.g., 1,250 + 1,200 + 1,125 = 3,575 LBS).
5. Add each moment together (e.g., 150,000 + 144,000 + -50,625 = 243,375).
6. Divide the total moment by the total weight (e.g., 243,375 / 3,575 = 68.07" CG).
7. The aircraft CG range is found on the TCDS or a given value. If the CG range is 64"-70" on this aircraft, then the CG is within range.
